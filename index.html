DRIBBLING TECHNIQUES

No1.SPEED DRIBBLING

Speed Dribbling: Used in open grass. Lock your ankle, point your toes down, and push the ball 3 to 5 feet ahead using your laces. The goal is to keep the ball in your natural sprinting stride without slowing down your pace.

No.2 The Stepover

The Stepover: Loop your foot over the front of the ball from inside to outside. Plant that foot hard and drop your shoulder to fake a lateral burst. When the defender shifts their weight, use the outside of your opposite foot to flick the ball the other way. Successfully outmanoeuvering the defender

No 3.La Croqueta

La Croqueta: Wait for the defender to lunge. Slide the ball horizontally across your body with the inside of one foot, fluidly hopping to catch and push it vertically forward with the inside of your other foot.

No 4.The Cruyff Turn

The Cruyff Turn: Wind up to aggressively fake a cross or shot, forcing the defender to turn or jump. Instead, use the inside of your foot to chop the ball backward behind your planted leg, pivot 180 degrees, and accelerate away.

No 5. The Elastico

The Elastico: A rapid, single-foot flick. Push the ball diagonally outward with your pinky toe to make the defender lean, then instantly snap your ankle around the ball to push it back inward with the inside of the same foot.
